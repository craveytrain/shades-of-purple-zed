## Provenance

This theme is a port for the [Zed](https://zed.dev/) editor of **Shades of
Purple**, originally created by Ahmad Awais for VSCode:
https://github.com/ahmadawais/shades-of-purple-vscode (MIT licensed).

It was built starting from [irmhonde/shades-of-purple-theme](https://github.com/irmhonde/shades-of-purple-theme),
an existing MIT-licensed Zed port of the same theme by Isheunopa Ralph Mhonde,
then adjusted to match Michael Cravey's personal customizations of Shades of
Purple (captured in his Nova editor theme) for both the regular and Super
Dark variants.
